# Enterprise website and service-architecture research used for AXNOVATECH V78

Research checked against official company pages on 17 November 2026. The purpose is to learn from information architecture and trust patterns, not to copy brand assets, page layouts, wording or proprietary content.

## TCS
Official sources:
- https://www.tcs.com/
- https://www.tcs.com/what-we-do.

Observed patterns:
- A clear top-level separation between industries, services, and products/platforms.
- The homepage connects a high-level positioning statement to service capabilities and industry selection.
- Product/platform portfolio is presented separately from service delivery.
- Customer stories, research and news are used as evidence and ongoing proof of expertise.

AXNOVATECH application:
- Keep the five business divisions as the main brand architecture.
- Maintain separate service, industry, product and case-study journeys.
- Add client stories only when there are real projects and permission to publish.

## Infosys
Official sources:
- https://www.infosys.com/
- https://www.infosys.com/services.html
- https://www.infosys.com/industries/

Observed patterns:
- Services are grouped into understandable capability themes rather than one undifferentiated list.
- Industry pages help visitors navigate by their business context.
- The services hub connects offerings with success stories and thought leadership.
- Named platforms and solution brands are distinguished from service capabilities.

AXNOVATECH application:
- Use dedicated pillar pages and capability lists.
- Keep industry pages as a separate path for sector-specific requirements.
- Treat proposed product names as concepts until they are validated, built and launched.

## Accenture
Official sources:
- https://www.accenture.com/en/services
- https://www.accenture.com/en/about/reinvention-services

Observed patterns:
- Capabilities are framed around business reinvention and outcomes, not just technology labels.
- AI/data, cloud, cybersecurity, digital engineering, ecosystem partners and technology transformation are visible capability areas.
- Industry expertise and ecosystem partnerships are connected to the service proposition.
- Recognition and research appear as proof points, with context.

AXNOVATECH application:
- Explain the business outcome for every service.
- Keep partnerships and integrations as a distinct capability.
- Publish awards, certifications, customer reviews and measurable results only after they are independently verified.

## Resulting V78 information architecture
1. AXNOVA DIGITAL — websites, applications and software engineering.
2. AXNOVA AI — AI assistants, automation and intelligent business systems.
3. AXNOVA CLOUD — cloud, DevOps, infrastructure and cybersecurity.
4. AXNOVA ENTERPRISE — enterprise platforms, integration and technology partnerships.
5. AXNOVA PRODUCTS — an in-house product-development pillar.

Shared site journeys:
- Industries
- Services and capabilities
- Products and solutions
- Work and case studies
- Insights
- Company, people and careers
- Contact and project enquiry

## Trust and compliance principles
- Do not publish fabricated employee profiles, customer logos, Google ratings, awards, certifications or numerical performance claims.
- A case study must describe an actual delivered engagement and be approved for publication.
- A product should be described as a concept, beta or launched offering accurately.
- Payment-related products require legal, banking, payment-network and regulatory review before they are marketed as available payment services.
- A polished website cannot guarantee Google Ads approval or search rankings. Those depend on policy compliance, account setup, landing-page quality and ongoing campaign work.
